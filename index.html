Frequently Asked Questions

Good to Know

Everything you need to plan your perfect backwater journey.

Q: When do the houseboats operate?

A: Cruising takes place between 12:00 PM and 5:30 PM. In accordance with local government regulations to protect fishermen's nets, houseboats anchor in a serene location from 5:30 PM until 8:00 AM the following morning.

Q: Is air conditioning available all day?

A: Deluxe boats provide AC in the bedrooms from 9:00 PM to 6:00 AM. Premium and Luxury suites offer full 24-hour AC throughout the boat.

Q: How do I get to the boarding point?

A: Our private jetty is located at Finishing Point, Alappuzha, easily accessible by taxi or auto-rickshaw from Alleppey Railway Station (4 km) or Cochin International Airport (85 km).
